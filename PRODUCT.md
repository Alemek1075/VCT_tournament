# VCT Hub

## Register
product (the home hero and team landing pages borrow brand moves)

## Users
Valorant esports fans following the 2026 Champions Tour. Typical moment: a second monitor next to a match stream, evening, dim room, checking scores, the bracket or a player's stats between rounds. Also course graders clicking through every feature.

## Product purpose
Schedule, results, teams, players and events of VCT 2026 in one place, plus the extras built for the course: live scores, pick'em on chain, aim trainer, shared board, docs, AI agent.

## Brand personality
Sharp, competitive, broadcast-grade. It should feel like it belongs next to valorantesports.com and playvalorant.com without copying their assets: near-black warm neutrals, VALORANT red, hard corners, condensed uppercase headings, quick confident motion.

## Anti-references
- Generic Bootstrap dark admin templates.
- Neon-on-black "gamer" sites with RGB glows everywhere.
- SaaS landing clichés: gradient text, glass cards, hero metrics.

## Principles
1. Scores and names first. Decoration never competes with data.
2. Motion confirms state: hover, press, reveal, live. 150-450 ms, ease-out only.
3. One accent. Red marks actions, live state and the current selection.
4. Works with JS off and with prefers-reduced-motion.

## Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast for text, visible focus ring on everything clickable, reduced-motion fallback, keyboard reachable command palette.
